On 13 April 2025 I flew to Tokyo for a two-week business trip. April is cherry blossom season, so on the plane I was already picturing the whole city glowing pink, but I arrived a bit late, most of the blossoms had fallen, and the streets were lined with cherry trees already in leaf. Luckily a few late bloomers were still flowering, so I quickly snapped a few shots, which means I've got blossom-season photos to show off after all.

On my last trip to Japan I went to Kansai with its ancient temples, but this time it was Tokyo, with a much faster pace of life. Every morning I'd see streams of people in suits walking briskly to the train stations, everyone on time, everyone orderly, and blending into that crowd I felt a few notches more professional myself hehe.

By day I worked very seriously, okay. The office here is full of satellite and rocket models, true space-industry style, and just looking at them was motivating. I learned so much from the way Japanese people work: everything neat, careful and prepared down to the last detail, and there's a lot I want to bring home and apply.

In the evenings, little Longpeoo turned into a tourist again, wandering all over Tokyo. The red and white Tokyo Tower stands out in the middle of the city, the red-brick Tokyo Station glows beautifully, tucked among the skyscrapers, and then there's Senso-ji in Asakusa with its five-storey pagoda shining bright at night. The funniest moment was walking down the street and seeing a whole convoy of Mario Karts zooming along the actual road. I stood there stunned for a few seconds before I managed to pull out my phone and take a photo lol.

On 26 April I flew back to Vietnam. My two trips to Japan were completely different experiences, once as a tourist and once as someone there to work. But each time I came home with the same thing: wanting to live with more discipline and more kindness every day.
