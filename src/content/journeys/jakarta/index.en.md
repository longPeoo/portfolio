This business trip lasted more than a week, and I only worked from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., because that's just how work goes over there. Every evening I'd slip out to explore. I love learning about culture; I want to know what people eat, what they drink, what they do for fun and how they make a living.

I was also really lucky that my partner loves to share too. His name is Auril, and I'm dedicating this whole post to him, because he truly is someone I consider a friend, even though he's from another country. Thank you so much, Auril (this post will be translated into English, but I don't know whether you'll ever get to read it haha).

Auril is a PM at the partner company. He handles a huge amount of work on his own and knows a great deal across many different fields. Being older, he has a lot of experience, from work to life in general, but once we were out of the office I only wanted to talk about everyday life around us.

Auril told me some really interesting things:

- In Indonesia everything is cheap, from the standard of living and everyday expenses to buying a house
- If you're in Jakarta you'll only see cats; if you want to see dogs, you have to go to PEAK beach
- Muslims here don't eat pork, so for that you have to go to places where Chinese people live

There's so much more, but I've made a vlog for this journey, so remember to watch it
