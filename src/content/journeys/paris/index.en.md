Yes, Paris is truly beautiful, France is truly beautiful, Europe is so beautiful. That kid was curious about everything and found everything new and strange. Actually, after all these years, whatever I come across now, I still keep a little of that childlike wonder for everything.

I didn't live in Paris; where I lived was a whole two hours from Paris by train, so I only went up to Paris now and then. But I don't like Paris. It's beautiful, for sure, but when you just don't like something, you just don't.

Still, Paris was the first place to welcome me, and the last to see me off from Europe, so I'm still very attached to it. Some day not far off, I'll grab my backpack and come back to visit this city. I still owe Disneyland Paris a trip, after all hehe
