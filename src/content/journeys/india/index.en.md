This trip to India lasted 10 days and was split into two very different halves. On the first day I arrived in Rishikesh, a spiritual town on the banks of the Ganges known as the yoga capital of the world. Almost the entire city is vegetarian, so that evening I spent hours just hunting for a meal with meat, starving but still laughing because I found myself so ridiculous hehe.

On the second day I travelled up to Pipalkoti, and on the third the trek officially began. The trail passes through Tugasi village and winds through towering forests; the further you go, the higher you climb and the thinner and colder the air gets. At night we slept in tents in the mountains, shivering all over, but in return I got to watch the sunset paint the whole Himalayan range purple, more beautiful than words can say.

Day four was the climb to Pangarchulla Peak, and on day five we conquered Kuari Pass. Everything was blanketed in white snow, my legs ached, I was gasping for breath, and at times I just wanted to plop down and never get up again. But then, when the first light of dawn set the summit glowing red as if it were on fire, I finally understood why people put themselves through so much to get all the way up here.

Back down from the mountains, I returned to New Delhi, and the first thing I did was go and buy medicine. Buying medicine in India is quite the experience, everyone, just watch the vlog and you'll see. After that I wandered all over the city, stopping at the Red Fort, India Gate and Humayun's Tomb, then got lost in crowded streets full of blaring horns, with people and cows sharing the same road, chaotic and yet somehow it all works. That's India for you.

The last stop was Agra, where I rode a tuk-tuk to the Taj Mahal, one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. In my Italy post I said the Colosseum was the first wonder I had ever seen with my own eyes, so now I'm at 2 out of 7, everyone. The white marble mausoleum was more beautiful than any photo I had ever seen, and that night back at the hotel I hung up the Vietnamese flag to celebrate.

So, hardship or bliss? Hardship for sure, but bliss for sure too. I made two vlogs about this journey: episode 1, the trek, is right below, and you can find episode 2, about New Delhi and the Taj Mahal, on my channel.
