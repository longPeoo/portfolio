Shanghai is, of course, the largest city in China: vast yet modern, while somehow still keeping its old-world charm, with buildings that look weathered and sometimes bear a British imprint.

My favourite spot was the Bund. On one side of the Huangpu River stands a row of grand European-style buildings dating back a century, and on the other is Lujiazui with the Shanghai Tower, the tallest building in China. In the evening, the lights turn both banks a brilliant gold, and standing in between I felt as if I were standing between two centuries, the past on one side and the future on the other. (an AI wrote this paragraph, but I liked it so I kept it hehe)

This will also be a city in my memories, maybe beautiful, maybe not quite, depending on the moment and on how I see it. But whatever, I came back having learned a lot and having seen plenty of things Vietnam could adopt and develop further.

Even just walking down the street I saw connections to my field, even riding in a ride-hailing car I saw connections to it, I guess my occupational hazard is pretty serious by now lol. But it's still worth the trip, and if I got the chance, well, I probably should come back. It still has its old charm and its modernity; I only dislike it because it's a rival, but respect is still respect.
