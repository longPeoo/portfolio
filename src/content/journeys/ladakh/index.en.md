I don't want to write too much about this journey, since I've already made two whole vlogs about it, which tells you just how much I love Ladakh.

The people here, I have to say, touched the very bottom of my heart: the Indians, the Tibetans and the people of mixed Indian and Tibetan heritage. Oh, they're wonderful, with their honesty, their hospitality, their courtesy and their generosity. That's how I learned that the news sometimes reflects nothing at all; you have to travel to faraway places to truly understand the people, the culture and the way of life there.

As for the scenery... no need to say more, just watch the vlogs and you'll see all of Ladakh. But each of us feels things in our own way; maybe I find it beautiful and you don't. So if you ever get the chance, do come here and feel it for yourself.
