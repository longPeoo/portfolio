On 12 March 2000 I let out my very first cry, and that was the day my longest journey began. Every other trip has a return date, but this one doesn't: each passing day adds one more day, and the number will keep growing until my journey of exploring the World comes to an end, or until I run out of money to pay for this domain, lol.

I've been to quite a few countries by now, from France, the capital of fashion, to Belgium, the kingdom of chocolate, to dreamy Switzerland, majestic India and Japan, the land of the rising sun, from Asia to Europe and even all the way to Australia, but there's always one place where I belong. Vietnam.

Vietnam has the most beautiful seas. The sea in central Vietnam is crystal clear, round basket boats lie sunbathing on the sand, fishing boats fill the whole bay, and sitting on the seawall watching the sunset makes you forget all your tiredness.

Then there are Vietnam's mountains and forests, and I'm crazy about climbing mountains. For no particular reason, I just love the feeling of conquering them, and I've always loved being surrounded by majestic Mother Nature, because after all, even though we're born to our parents, our roots still come from nature.

And Vietnam is also where I grew up, where I work and where I give my all. I'm an engineer working in the space industry, and I've even been on VTV1, well, actually, not just once, quite a few times by now. I feel that I've lived a truly brilliant life, at least to me, and I'll keep living so that when I'm old there's nothing in this world I'll regret, feel remorse for or grieve over, whether it's something I did or something I never did.

This journey has never had an end date, and I hope it never will. I'll keep going, keep wandering everywhere, and I'll travel to every last corner of Vietnam to love this country with all my heart.
