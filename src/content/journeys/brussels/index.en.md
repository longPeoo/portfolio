On 6 May 2023 I grabbed my backpack and boarded a train from France to Belgium, officially starting the first solo trip of my life. It was the first time I'd planned everything from A to Z, from booking train tickets and finding a place to sleep to working out how much each meal would cost. Sitting on the train, I kept opening my phone to check and recheck the itinerary as if it might run away hehe.

Brussels isn't very big, everyone, but when I stepped into the Grand Place in the evening, I froze. The square is surrounded by ornately carved old buildings glowing golden under the lights, and I kept turning around, then turning around again, taking photo after photo without knowing which angle was the most beautiful.

In Brussels, you absolutely have to visit the little peeing boy, Manneken Pis. I'd heard about him forever, but when I got there he turned out to be teeny tiny, and I had to squeeze in among the crowd holding up their phones. The day I visited he was even wearing a full gold outfit; I heard he has a wardrobe of more than a thousand costumes, way fancier than me lol.

I also stopped by a museum and wandered down into the metro stations, and there was art everywhere. There's one quote on a wall that has stuck with me: "The piano ain't got no wrong notes". As in, there are no wrong notes on a piano, and travelling alone is the same: if you take a wrong turn, just treat it as exploring a little more.

Just three short days, but this was the first time I learned to look after myself in a foreign country. Back home I felt so much more confident, and less than two weeks later I'd already grabbed my backpack and headed to the Netherlands.
