On 7 November I landed in Almaty, and the first thing I did was head out to look at the yellow leaves. Almaty is so beautiful this time of year, everyone, with yellow and orange trees on both sides of the road, and when a green bus drives past it looks just like a postcard. In the late afternoon the sky turns bright orange, and I just kept wandering around on foot.

Big Almaty Lake sits up in the mountains, not far from the city. The water is turquoise and the mountains around it are covered in white snow. Standing in front of all that I suddenly got shy, had no idea how to pose, so I just stood there smiling hehe.

The next day we drove out to Charyn Canyon. People call it the little brother of the Grand Canyon in the US. I haven't been to the Grand Canyon so I can't compare, but this little brother is already gorgeous. Layer upon layer of red rock, and standing on the edge looking down I felt like I was on Mars.

Then it was the turn of Kolsay Lake and Kaindy Lake. Kaindy is so strange: in the middle of the lake there's a whole sunken forest, bare tree trunks standing straight up out of the water. The story goes that over a hundred years ago an earthquake caused a landslide that blocked the stream, the water rose into a lake, and the trees have been standing there ever since. A little eerie, but beautiful in a way I can't quite explain.

After the lakes we all got back in the van and drove straight to the border to cross into Kyrgyzstan. I'll tell you about that in the next post.
