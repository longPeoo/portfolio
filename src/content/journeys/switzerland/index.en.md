In Switzerland, sometimes the thing that made me think the hardest wasn't where to go, but what to eat so my budget would still stretch to the days ahead. I'm not too fussy about food anyway, as long as I'm full and alive, so that was that, because Zurich was only the warm-up.

What I was looking forward to most in Switzerland were the mountain regions of the Alps. So the journey continued to Interlaken, a town nestled between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz and surrounded by majestic mountains. From there, I spent time exploring famous spots like Harder Kulm, Wengen, Grindelwald and, above all, Jungfraujoch. Jungfraujoch is often promoted as the Top of Europe, with a railway station at about 3,454 metres above sea level, but I still took a photo higher than the mountains, how cool is that.

Oh, and after that the journey also took in Lake Brienz, the village of Iseltwald and the steam railway up the Brienz Rothorn.

Switzerland is famous for a railway network that carries travellers through valleys, along mountainsides and across picture-perfect landscapes. So here, sometimes the train ride itself is already the experience. Before leaving Switzerland, I also stopped by Lucerne and the Mount Titlis area, then returned to Zurich to get ready for the next leg.

If I had to sum up Switzerland in a few words, I'd say it's a country where nature has done its job far too well, and everything is just amazing. Trust me, here all you need to do is raise your camera and press the shutter to bring home a great photo, I didn't edit a thing
