Singapore is a tiny country, so tiny you can almost cover it all in one loop, yet it's so clean, green and orderly that it amazed me. Not a single scrap of litter on the streets, and greenery everywhere, climbing from the ground all the way up to the roofs of the high-rise buildings, truly the city in a garden, as people like to call it.

But for someone like me who isn't into cities and modern urban life, hmmmm, I prefer nature: mountains taller than buildings, rivers and lakes calmer than streams of traffic, and trees to embrace and shelter me rather than crowds of people.

If it's just to go and see it, well, I've seen it now, since this was my second time, but the first doesn't count because I was little back then and didn't have impressions of my own yet
