We crossed from Kazakhstan into Kyrgyzstan by road through the border post. It's a long way, but looking out of the window it was all meadows and free-roaming horses, so I never got bored. That night we stayed in Karakol, a small town near Lake Issyk-Kul.

The next morning we climbed into a Russian UAZ to head into the Altyn Arashan valley. The road was horribly bumpy, we were bouncing around inside like on a rollercoaster, but once we got there it was so worth it: sunshine, snowy mountains all around, and a stream roaring below.

Jeti-Oguz means Seven Bulls, a row of huge red cliffs. I stood there counting for ages and still couldn't find all seven, I guess you need an artist's eye lol.

The best part was the eagle hunting show. The hunter put the eagle on my arm, and when it spread its wings it was almost as big as me, so heavy my arm was shaking. I was scared and trying to smile for a nice photo at the same time.

On the last day we stopped by Ala Archa, all covered in white snow under a deep blue sky. The day after, we did a loop around Bishkek and then took the van back to Almaty, wrapping up the Central Asia trip. See you again, -stan brothers.
