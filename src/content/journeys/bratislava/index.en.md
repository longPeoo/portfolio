Honestly, this really is one of Europe's forgotten cities. In a dazzling Europe of glamorous Paris, timeless Vienna, lovesick Prague and magnificent Budapest, the capital Bratislava doesn't stand out much and is always left off the famous travel itineraries.

But I don't like following the crowd, so I went anyway, and even though I was only passing through because it was on the way, it was enough to make me want to come back again. This place still gives visitors the feeling of living several centuries ago, with whole streets still paved in cobblestones, winding through the heart of the city.

Maybe I don't need glitz and grandeur either; all I need is a peaceful patch of sky and the chance to stroll through the streets without being squeezed by crowds (tourist crowds, no thanks; crowds of locals, I like, even though I'm a tourist too haha).

But that's the thing: because this little buddy is surrounded by big buddies like Hungary, Austria and Czechia, people tend to forget about it. If you ever get the chance, don't forget to stop by; it's truly simple and not very grand, but it's enough to remember.
