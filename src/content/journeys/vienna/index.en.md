At 11:37 a.m. on 27 August 2023 I boarded the train in Bratislava, and by 12:44 I was already in Vienna, even quicker than getting from my place to Paris, honestly. After checking in at the homestay I tossed my suitcase in a corner and headed straight out, because on this whole trip I had just one afternoon and one morning for the Austrian capital.

Vienna is called the music capital of the world, where Mozart and Beethoven once lived and composed. Walking past the State Opera, I still had to stop and gaze at it for a long time; such a big, beautiful, elegant building that just looking at it made me feel like I was in a European period film.

If you've noticed, every photo from Vienna has an ice cream in it, and that's not a mistake, it's a style hehe. Standing in front of St. Stephen's Cathedral, with its gorgeous green and yellow patterned roof, ice cream in hand, then walking over to the Karlskirche for another one. The weather was beautiful and sunny that day, and eating ice cream while gazing at churches, I felt that life was enough just like that.

Vienna is also a city of palaces, from the Hofburg, where the Habsburg dynasty lived for several hundred years, to Schönbrunn and the Belvedere. I liked the Belvedere best: the pool in front of the palace is as still as a mirror, and from this side you can capture the whole palace and the sky in one shot, no editing needed at all.

The next morning, 28 August, I dragged my suitcase back to the station to head to Prague. To me, Vienna is a neat, clean and gentle city. I stayed less than a day yet ate so much ice cream, and that's enough to call it a complete trip.
