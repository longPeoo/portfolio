Here, as a kid just starting to nurture big ambitions, I got to test myself in my own field. I worked for a French government research institute, researching "ocean colour", but let's save that for later! Coming to dreamy France, of course, as someone far from home I felt sad, missing home and my friends, but I still enjoyed life, and whenever I had free time I'd slip off to another country.

Where I lived was close to the sea, so it was so chill. Every day I'd walk out to look at the sea, and sometimes I even walked home from work. Oh, I really miss life over there, but I still love Vietnam, so I had to come back to serve my homeland and my dear fellow Vietnamese, right?

This was both my home and my workplace, where I worked as an intern and a researcher, learning how to become a scientist. It's also the place that witnessed my endless homesickness, and the place that nourished my soul. I loved the gentle life here by the sea; everything was wonderful, I just wasn't used to life under capitalism.

Anyway, I'm truly grateful.
