It was supposed to be a lavender-viewing tour, but I missed the lavender for a few reasons, so having come all this way, I went to explore the city instead. I love wandering; often I don't have any plan at all, because I like the feeling of things not going my way, since whichever way they go, I'll still do it anyway, right hehe.

Wandering around the city alone has its charm too. This is a port city, and I visited every church I possibly could, walking dozens of kilometres every day. I came over to study and ended up losing a whole 10 kg. I look like a model, but I don't care that much about appearance; there are many other things worth caring about more, and as long as my legs are strong enough to keep walking, that's all that matters!
