A small country, but far from ordinary. If you'd asked me before I came here which was the richest country in Europe, I'd probably have said Switzerland or Germany, but no, it's Luxembourg.

A small country, rich and far more powerful than people imagine. So small that if you look at a map of Europe without paying close attention, you might not even notice where it is.

And yet Luxembourg has one of the highest GDPs per capita in the world, is a major financial centre in Europe and is also home to several European Union institutions.

This little buddy is tucked right between three countries, Belgium, Germany and France, so here you can speak pretty much any language haha. English works too, of course, but not everyone speaks it.

Before coming to Luxembourg, I assumed a country famous for its wealth would have lots of modern buildings, luxury shopping districts or streets that are always crowded. But Luxembourg City gave me a rather different, kind of unusual feeling. The city has cobbled streets, old sandstone buildings, small squares and neighbourhoods perched on hills and down in valleys.

I was completely smitten; travelling alone has its own charm. I took my time wandering everywhere at a leisurely pace, admiring every little street corner, because here there's none of the hustle of Paris, the famous canals of Amsterdam or the vast squares of Brussels. But I still love it
