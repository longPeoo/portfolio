I have to admit this is an incredibly charming country, and the people here are wonderful too. Everyone is so gentle, or maybe I just haven't met enough people yet, but that's what I know for now.

Dutch architecture is also very different from everywhere else, and the city is divided up by countless bridges spanning its waterways.

The city of tulips. I like this flower too; in case you didn't know, I have a tulip tattoo on my arm. It's quite pretty, but the story behind it shouldn't ever come up again in my life hehe, I just like beautiful things. Oh, and when I went, the flower season had just ended, which was a bit sad, so that's all the more reason to come back someday.

Here, bars and coffeeshops don't sell beer, alcohol or coffee, they sell weed hehe, so don't get confused when you visit
