At 10:35 a.m. on 28 August 2023 I boarded the train in Vienna and sat for more than four hours before reaching Prague, and the moment I stepped off at the station it started raining, before I'd even had a chance to say hello. But Prague's main railway station is gorgeous, everyone, with a dome painted with patterns and intricately carved walls. I stood there gazing up for ages and forgot all about the fact that I hadn't brought an umbrella.

For my first half-day in Prague I braved the rain to visit Old Town Square, Wenceslas Square and the National Museum, and even stopped by the Sapa market. Sapa is the Vietnamese market in Prague, and hearing Vietnamese spoken under European skies after nearly two weeks of wandering suddenly warmed my heart so much.

The must-try food in Prague is the trdelník chimney cake: dough wrapped around a stick and turned over hot coals, then sprinkled with sugar and cinnamon that fills the whole street corner with its aroma. They even stuff it with ice cream, so there I was, walking through the rain holding an ice cream pastry, a true ice cream lover whatever the weather. And here's the funny part: I thought only Amsterdam had cannabis shops, but right in the middle of a Prague street there was one glowing bright green. I took a photo and moved on, I didn't go in, okay.

The morning of 29 August was my last outing. I went up to Prague Castle, admired St. Vitus Cathedral, then walked across Charles Bridge. It was still drizzling, so there were hardly any people around; the old stone bridge with its statues on both sides faded into the rain, looking a little melancholy and yet somehow romantic. Maybe that's why people call it lovesick Prague.

At 12:15 p.m. I flew back to Paris, closing out a 15-day trip through seven countries: Switzerland, Italy, Vatican City, Hungary, Slovakia, Austria and Czechia. Sitting on the plane looking back, I realised how much ground I had really covered. Tired, yes, and my wallet got a lot thinner, but in return I got a whole sky full of memories, and it was so worth it.
