I was in Australia for more than a week, enough time to work and to explore when night fell. Back then Vietnam was getting ready for autumn, but over here it was spring, because Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere, so everything is the opposite of home.

Stepping into the congress, I was like a kid lost in a toy shop. That's why they say men never grow up, their toys just keep getting bigger and more expensive haha. I'm no exception: looking at the rocket engines, the landers and the satellites from different countries, I was completely smitten, and I promised myself I'd work hard so the field can grow in Vietnam, even though I'm only a grain of sand in a vast desert.

The moment I was proudest of was raising the red flag with the yellow star high, together with everyone, right in the middle of the congress. Among so many space powers, Vietnam was still there, still striving step by step, and little Longpeoo got to play a part in it too. Just thinking about it makes me feel both proud and pretty awesome.

Once the work was done, of course it was time to go and explore. Wandering is in every cell of my body, so I still dragged myself around everywhere. This time I travelled with my head of department, someone I'm truly fond of: not just a boss, but a mentor, a big brother and a friend who shows me the way and has taught me so many things.

Only when you travel far do you see how vast the world is, and how much work our country's space industry still has ahead of it. But that's okay, if we just keep going one step at a time, we'll get there somehow, right everyone?
