A group project carried out in Quy Nhon under the guidance of Assoc. Prof. Nguyễn Thị Hồng Vân (USTH) and Dr Cao Văn Sơn (ICISE). A topic that sounds straight out of a science fiction film: using particles from space to image the inside of a stone structure.

## What I did

- Studied neutrinos: their history, phenomenology, interactions, detection methods, sources and applications.
- Hands-on hardware work: characterising multi-pixel photon counters (MPPCs) and using the DRS4 to digitise signals.
- Simulated in G4Beamline how muons can be used to detect the Queen's Chamber inside a pyramid.

## That same year

In 2020, my dear friends and I completed the Vietnam School on Neutrinos at ICISE and won the Outstanding Final Presentation award at VSON 2020.
