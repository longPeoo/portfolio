The Vietnam International Defense Expo 2024 took place from 19 to 22 December 2024 at Gia Lam Airport in Hanoi, organised by the Ministry of National Defence. I was there as an exhibitor, staffing VEGASTAR's booth.

## What VEGASTAR brought to the expo

At the expo, VEGASTAR launched its comprehensive Digital Army solution suite (Vega Digital Army), made up of integrated systems built on digital platforms and AI technology developed by Vietnamese engineers. The corner international visitors stopped by most was the digital soldier, a model of a soldier carrying AI-integrated wearable devices.

## What I did there

I staffed the booth, presented our solutions to domestic and international visitors, answered questions and connected with interested organisations. I developed the products myself, so I guess I know them pretty well, which is why I was just there to sell them. As for what the products actually are, maybe in another post, or maybe not, because sometimes you have to keep a little secret, right? hehe

## What I remember most

Standing in the middle of a defence expo of international stature, watching Vietnamese technology on display next to major corporations from around the world, I felt small, and I felt that I needed to learn, research and develop so much more. But it also strengthened my belief that I can do it!
