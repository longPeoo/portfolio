The IAC (International Astronautical Congress) is the world's largest annual space conference. In 2025, the 76th IAC took place in Sydney from 29 September to 3 October under the theme "Sustainable Space: Resilient Earth", bringing together organisations from more than 80 countries, including NASA, SpaceX, Airbus and Thales.

## What VegaCosmos brought to the IAC

VegaCosmos, a subsidiary of VEGASTAR, presented two core technologies developed by Vietnamese engineers:

- GEOHUB AI Engine: a smart geospatial platform that combines artificial intelligence, data analytics and real-time reporting to support decision-making.
- GEOAI – GEOGPT: an AI tool for geospatial data.

Visitors and international experts watched live demonstrations of applications in agriculture, forestry, maritime, energy and smart cities.

## What I did there

I travelled with the VegaCosmos delegation, staffed the booth to present our products and chatted with visitors. The moment I remember most is the whole team raising the red flag with the yellow star right in the middle of the world's biggest space conference.
