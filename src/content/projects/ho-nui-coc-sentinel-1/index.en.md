The final internship project of my Bachelor's degree in Space and Applications at the University of Science and Technology of Hanoi (USTH), supervised by Dr Phạm Đức Bình. Nui Coc Lake in Thai Nguyen changes its water level with the seasons, which made it a perfect place to try out cloud-penetrating radar imagery.

## What I did

- Used Sentinel-1 radar imagery to monitor the water surface area of Nui Coc Lake with a local processing workflow in SNAP.
- Extracted the water surface from Sentinel-1 on Google Earth Engine to compare it with the local workflow.
- Used Sentinel-2 optical imagery to cross-check the results of the two methods.
