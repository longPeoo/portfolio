On 15 November 2024 in Ho Chi Minh City, the Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology (VAST) and the Vietnam National Space Center (VNSC) held a workshop presenting the application potential of LOTUSAT-1, Vietnam's remote sensing satellite, while also celebrating the 10th anniversary of the Space Technology Application Center in Ho Chi Minh City. VEGASTAR was a sponsor of the workshop.

## What is LOTUSAT-1?

LOTUSAT-1 is Vietnam's first synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite. Unlike optical satellites, radar can image day and night and see through clouds, which suits a country with as much cloud and rain as ours. It's also exactly the kind of data I work with every day to map land subsidence and landslides.

## What I did there

I was there as the representative of the sponsor, VEGASTAR, and took the podium to share my thoughts with the delegates. Meeting fellow professionals in Ho Chi Minh City and hearing about the problems waiting for radar data made my everyday work feel far more meaningful.
