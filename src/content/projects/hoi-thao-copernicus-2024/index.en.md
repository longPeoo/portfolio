On 12 June 2024, the Vietnam National Space Center (VNSC) and the Copernicus programme jointly held the workshop "Extending Copernicus User Uptake Activities in Vietnam" in Hanoi.

## Copernicus and me

Copernicus is the European Union's Earth observation programme, whose Sentinel satellites provide free data to the whole world. Almost every project I've worked on, from monitoring the water surface of Nui Coc Lake and assessing flooding in Can Tho to water quality in the Mekong River, has relied on Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 imagery. So getting to sit and listen to the very people behind the programme was a really special experience for me.

## What I took away

The workshop discussed how to bring Copernicus data closer to Vietnamese government agencies, research institutes and businesses. I came away with plenty of ideas for my work at the company, building lectures and automated image processing workflows.
