My Master's internship project, carried out at the LOG laboratory (Laboratoire d'Océanologie et de Géosciences) in Wimereux, France, under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Charles Verpoorter. It's also the time I lived in a lovely little seaside town, so read more in my [Boulogne log](/en/journeys/boulogne-sur-mer).

## The problem

The colour of river water tells you a lot about what is drifting in it. Using satellite imagery, I analysed the spatial and temporal variability of the Mekong River's water, separating the organic and inorganic fractions of the suspended particulate matter.

## What I did

- Characterised the organic and inorganic fractions of suspended particulate matter from Landsat-8 and Sentinel-2 imagery.
- Applied and compared several atmospheric correction algorithms, including Polymer, C2RCC, Sen2Cor and MAJA, to preprocess the entire dataset.
- Used neural network algorithms to estimate these parameters.
