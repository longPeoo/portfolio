The NASA International Space Apps Challenge is the world's largest space hackathon, where teams tackle real-world problems using data from NASA and its partner space agencies. In 2025, the challenge took place worldwide over two days, 4 and 5 October.

## What I did there

On 5 September 2025, I attended the kick-off ceremony of NASA Space Apps Challenge Hanoi 2025 at the U.S. Embassy in Hanoi. VegaCosmos was one of the partners supporting this year's Hanoi edition.

## Why I love this event

Back when I was a student, I used to tinker with free satellite data too, wondering how to turn it into something useful. Space Apps is exactly where young people get to do that in just two days, so standing alongside them as a supporter is a huge joy.
