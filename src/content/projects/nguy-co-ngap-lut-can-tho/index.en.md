An internship project at Hanoi University of Natural Resources and Environment (HUNRE), supervised by Dr Trương Vân Anh. Can Tho lies in the heart of the Mekong Delta, where flooding is woven into people's lives every flood season.

## What I did

- Combined land cover data (Sentinel-2), Sentinel-1 radar imagery and a digital elevation model (DEM) to map flood risk for Can Tho.
- Used Google Earth Engine to extract binary water maps from Sentinel-1 imagery.
- Learned more about hydrometeorology to understand the problem from the ground up.
