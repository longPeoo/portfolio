Knowing how to do something is one thing; teaching other people to do it is something else entirely. I was tasked with building the content for these courses, from the fundamentals to hands-on practice with the software.

## What I do

- Develop training programmes on remote sensing, remote sensing image processing, GIS and geospatial data processing.
- Write lectures and practical exercises tailored to each learner's level.
- Teach remote sensing, GIS, WebGIS and the use of AI agents in data processing.

## What I've learned

Every class pushes me to understand the material more deeply so I can explain it as simply as possible. Watching learners make their very first map with their own hands is the best feeling in teaching.
