Land subsidence and landslides are two silent but very real hazards in Vietnam, from the big cities to the northern mountains. Satellite radar can image the ground day and night and see through clouds, which makes it an ideal data source for tracking very small changes in the Earth's surface over time.

## What I do

- Automate the entire radar data processing chain, from downloading and preprocessing the images to exporting the results.
- Map land subsidence and landslides for provinces and cities, period by period.

## Why I love this project

This is the work where I see most clearly what remote sensing is really for: data coming back from space helps protect the very land I live on.
